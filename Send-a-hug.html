Frequently asked questions

Simple answers for visitors before they purchase.

Does the recipient get a physical item?

No. This page is for a digital hug card experience. It is meant to send encouragement, comfort, and support online.

Do I have to pay before sending the card?

Yes. The $10 contribution is completed through Stripe first. After payment, the thank-you page will help you share the gift code and link.

Will the email send automatically?

This version prepares the buyer and recipient details and makes sharing simple from the thank-you page. Fully automatic email delivery would need an added email service or automation later.

Can I customize the message?

Yes. Use the message box to write your own note. That message is saved with the gift details and can be shared with the recipient after payment.
